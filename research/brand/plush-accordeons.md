## 1. Why Plush

```
You've upgraded your shampoo, your conditioner, your serum. Everything but the water.

Your city adds chlorine to keep tap water safe on its way through miles of pipes. That's a
good thing. U.S. rules allow up to 4 milligrams of it per liter at your tap.

By the time it reaches your shower, it has done its job. Plush filters it out at the shower
head, right before the water reaches you.

No plumber. No tools. Your routine stays the same. The water doesn't.

City water in. Plush water out.
```

## 2. How It Works

```
Every Plush cartridge holds two stages of filtration:

• KDF-55: copper and zinc granules that turn free chlorine into chloride, the same chloride
  found in table salt. Made to keep working in hot water.
• Coconut-shell activated carbon: catches leftover chlorine and the smell that comes with it.

What it won't do: soften hard water. Removing calcium and magnesium takes a whole-home
softener, not a shower head. We'd rather tell you now than on day 30.

Filter capabilities refer to what the filter is designed to reduce, not to the guaranteed
presence of those substances in your water. Individual results vary. Not a medical device and
not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition.
```

## 3. Installs in 2 Minutes

```
If you can open a jar, you can install Plush.

• Fits the standard U.S. ½-inch shower arm, the one in almost every home and apartment.  [à confirmer : filetage 1/2" NPT]
• Unscrew your old shower head by hand. Stuck? A cloth and a wrench free it in seconds.
• Wrap the thread with the plumber's tape in the box.  [à confirmer : téflon fourni]
• Screw Plush on, hand-tight. Run the water for 30 seconds before your first shower.  [à confirmer : rinçage conseillé par le fournisseur]

Renting? Keep your old shower head in a drawer. Swap it back the day you move out.
```

```
  darker than it went in. We didn't make it brown. Your water did.

```
