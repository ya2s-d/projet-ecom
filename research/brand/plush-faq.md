## Les textes

```
1. Will Plush fit my shower?
Plush fits the standard U.S. ½-inch shower arm, the one in almost every home and apartment. If it doesn't fit yours, you have 60 days to send it back for a full refund.
```
`[à confirmer : filetage ½" NPT]`

```
2. How hard is it to install?
If you can open a jar, you can install Plush. Unscrew your old shower head by hand, then screw Plush on, hand-tight. No plumber, no tools, about two minutes. Old one stuck? Wrap it in a cloth and turn it with a wrench.
```

```
3. Will it lower my water pressure?
Any filter sits in the water's path, so we won't promise you more pressure. Here's what we can promise: if it doesn't feel right in your shower, you have 60 days to send it back for a full refund.
```
Version finale, après le test du seau (chiffres réels uniquement) :
```
We timed it. A 10-liter bucket filled in [X] seconds with a standard shower head, and in [Y] seconds with Plush. Same tap, same setting. And if it doesn't feel right in your shower, you have 60 days to send it back.
```

```
4. I'm renting. Can I still use it?
Yes. Plush doesn't change your plumbing. Keep your old shower head in a drawer and put it back the day you move out.
```

```
5. How often should I change the filter?
Every 60 days. Go by the calendar, not the color. With Plush Duo, your second filter is already in the box, so there's nothing to reorder.
```
`[à confirmer : durée de la cartouche]`

```
6. What if I don't love it?
Try Plush for 60 days. On day 59, if you don't love it, you get all your money back. Just email us at [EMAIL] and we'll take it from there.
```
`[à confirmer : adresse email · si le retour est à vos frais, ajouter « We cover return shipping. »]`

```
7. How long does shipping take?
Shipping is free in the U.S. and fully tracked. Your order arrives in 7 to 12 business days. You'll get your tracking link by email as soon as it ships.
```
`[à confirmer : Alaska, Hawaï, Porto Rico]`

```
8. Solo or Duo: which one should I get?
Plush Duo comes with a spare filter, so your next change is already covered. Same shower head, two filters, nothing to reorder. That's why it's our best value.
```

```
9. What's inside the filter?
Six layers: PP cotton, KDF-55, calcium sulfite, activated carbon, vitamin C beads, and a steel mesh that holds it all together. They're designed to reduce chlorine and catch sediment before the water reaches you. Individual results vary.
```
`[à confirmer : billes de vitamine C]`

```
10. Is Plush a water softener?
No, and we'd rather tell you now than on day 30. Removing the minerals in hard water takes a whole-home softener, not a shower head. Plush is made to reduce chlorine and catch sediment.
```

```
11. Why does my filter turn brown?
Depending on your water, your filter can come out darker than it went in. That's what it caught before the water reached you. We didn't make it brown. Your water did.
```

```
12. When will I notice a difference?
The first thing you may notice is the smell: no more pool smell in your shower. How your hair and skin feel is personal, so give it a few weeks. That's exactly why you get 60 days.
```
