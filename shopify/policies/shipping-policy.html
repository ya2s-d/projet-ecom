Free U.S. shipping

Every order ships free within the United States, with tracking.

Delivery time

Your order arrives in 7 to 12 business days from the day you place it (business days are Monday to Friday, excluding U.S. holidays). You'll get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships.

Where your order ships from

Plush ships from our fulfillment partner's international warehouse. Your tracking may show international transit before your package reaches the U.S. postal network. That's normal.

No customs fees

All import duties and customs fees are included in our prices. You won't pay anything on delivery.

Where we deliver

We ship to addresses in the United States. If we can't deliver to your address, we'll let you know and refund you in full before anything ships.

Late or lost packages

If your order hasn't arrived within 20 business days, or your tracking hasn't updated in 10 business days, email us. We'll send a replacement or refund you in full.

If your tracking says "delivered" but you can't find your package, check with your neighbors and your building's mail area, then email us within 7 days and we'll help you sort it out.

Wrong address

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email us right away. We can update it as long as your order hasn't shipped.
