60-day money-back guarantee

Try Plush for 60 days from the day it's delivered. If you don't love it, email us within those 60 days and you get all your money back.

To start a refund, email us at info.tryplush@gmail.com with your order number and a few words about what didn't work for you. We reply within 2 business days.

In most cases, you won't need to send anything back. If we do ask you to return the product, we'll email you a prepaid return label, so returning it costs you nothing.

How refunds are paid

Once your refund is approved, we process it within 3 business days to your original payment method. Depending on your bank, it can take 5 to 10 business days to show on your statement.

Damaged, defective or wrong item

If your Plush arrives damaged, doesn't work as it should, or isn't what you ordered, email us a photo within 60 days of delivery. We'll send you a free replacement or a full refund, your choice.

Leaking at the top? Unscrew Plush, check that the small rubber washer is sitting flat, then screw it back on hand-tight. Still leaking? Email us and we'll make it right.

Cancellations

You can cancel your order for a full refund as long as it hasn't shipped yet. Email us as soon as possible with your order number.

Limits

The 60-day money-back guarantee applies once per customer and per household, to orders placed on our website.
